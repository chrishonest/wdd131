Website Planning Document

This site plan defines the purpose, audience, content, visual design, navigation, and responsive layout for the D'ose Crust Bakery website.

Site Name

D'ose Crust Bakery

The name D'ose Crust Bakery represents a bakery focused on fresh bread and baked goods. The name is memorable and connects the business identity with the quality crust and freshness of its products.

Site Purpose and Goals

The purpose of the website is to present D'ose Crust Bakery online and provide customers with clear information about the bakery, its products, and how to make an inquiry. Visitors can explore products, read descriptions, and use the contact form for questions or order inquiries.

Site Goals

  • Present the bakery in a professional and welcoming way.
  • Display relevant information about available products.
  • Help customers discover products quickly.
  • Provide an easy way to make an inquiry.
  • Provide responsive navigation for mobile and desktop users.
  • Demonstrate JavaScript interaction using filtering, localStorage, DOM manipulation, and events.

Target Audience

The primary audience includes local customers, families, individuals, retailers, and other customers looking for fresh bread and baked products in Abuja.

Audience Needs

  • Quick access to product information.
  • Clear product descriptions and categories.
  • Simple navigation on phones and computers.
  • A clear method for asking questions or making inquiries.
  • A trustworthy, readable, and visually appealing bakery website.

Scenarios

Scenario 1

What types of bread and baked products are available at D'ose Crust Bakery?

Scenario 2

How can I contact D'ose Crust Bakery to ask about prices, availability, or placing an order?

Scenario 3

Which product category best matches the type of bread I want to buy?

Site Map

The website has three primary customer-facing pages and two supporting pages.

  • Home — index.html: introduction, featured products, benefits, and call to action.
  • Products — products.html: product cards, category filtering, and favorite-category localStorage feature.
  • Contact — contact.html: business information and inquiry form.
  • Thank You — thankyou.html: form-submission confirmation page.
  • References — references.html: project resources and image-source notes.

Website Content

Home Page

Hero image and welcome message, bakery introduction, featured products generated with JavaScript, three reasons to choose the bakery, and a contact call to action.

Products Page

Five product cards with local images, descriptions, categories, a category filter, and a favorite-category feature saved with localStorage.

Contact Page

Bakery location and business information plus a form for full name, email address, subject, and message.

Color Scheme

Brown and cream tones create a warm bakery identity while maintaining readable contrast.

#6B3100 Primary #C76B00 Secondary #F8F4EE Light #2B1B12 Dark #F2C078 Accent

  • Primary brown: header, footer, and major branding areas.
  • Secondary orange-brown: buttons, active navigation, and interactive elements.
  • Light cream: primary page background.
  • Dark brown: body text and hero background.
  • Accent gold: focus indicators and highlights.

Typography

Arial, Helvetica, Sans-Serif

Arial is the primary typeface because it is familiar, readable, and widely available. Helvetica and the generic sans-serif family provide reliable fallbacks.

Heading Style

Headings use larger sizes and strong contrast to establish visual hierarchy.

Body Style

Body text uses a readable size, comfortable line height, and adequate spacing for mobile and desktop reading.

Branding and Element Styling

  • Use the bakery name consistently on all pages.
  • Maintain the brown-and-cream color palette.
  • Use consistent navigation, buttons, spacing, and rounded cards.
  • Use descriptive alternative text for informative images.
  • Use visible keyboard focus states.
  • Use clear labels and adequate spacing in forms.

Product Cards

Product cards use white backgrounds, rounded corners, internal spacing, subtle shadows, and consistent image dimensions.

Buttons and Links

Buttons use the secondary color with white text. Hover and keyboard focus states provide clear feedback.

Responsive Design

The site follows a mobile-first approach. On smaller screens, navigation uses a menu button and content stacks into a single column. At 700 pixels and above, navigation becomes horizontal and the hero, product, and benefits sections use multiple columns.

Accessibility, Performance, and JavaScript Plan

  • Use semantic HTML elements and logical heading structure.
  • Provide descriptive image alternative text and explicit image dimensions.
  • Use visible keyboard focus indicators and labeled form controls.
  • Lazy-load non-hero product images and prioritize the hero image.
  • Use JavaScript functions, arrays, objects, array methods, DOM manipulation, events, conditional logic, and localStorage.
  • Keep the site lightweight by using local images and no unnecessary external libraries.
