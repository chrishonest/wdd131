Site Name

D'ose Crust Bakery

The name D'ose Crust Bakery represents a bakery that produces fresh bread and other baked goods. The name is memorable and connects directly to the bakery's focus on freshly baked products with a delicious crust.

Site Purpose

The D'ose Crust Bakery website will provide information about the bakery and its products. Visitors will be able to learn about the different types of bread and baked goods available, discover featured products, and contact the bakery for product inquiries or orders.

Scenarios

What types of bread and baked products are available at D'ose Crust Bakery?

How can I contact D'ose Crust Bakery to ask about prices, availability, or placing an order?

Color Schema

Golden Brown - #C68642

Golden brown will be used for buttons, navigation, accents, and highlights.

Dark Brown - #5C4033

Dark brown will be used for headings, important text, and the footer.

Cream - #FFF8E7

Cream will be used as the main background color.

Typography

Merriweather

Merriweather will be used for the website name, headings, and section titles.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, navigation, buttons, forms, and other general website content.
